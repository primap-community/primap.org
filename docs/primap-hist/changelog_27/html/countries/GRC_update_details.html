Notes on data changes

Here we list notes explaining important emissions changes for the country.

  • Data from BTR1 has been replaced by data from the 2025 National Inventory Submission. This leads to changes in several sectors and gases which are small in terms of total emissions.
    • The main change for 2023 is a 5.4% reduction in energy CO2 emissions where 2023 growth rates in the CR data are lower than the growth rates in EI 2024 used for extrapolation in PRIMAP-hist v2.6.1
    • N2O in the “other” sector is now non-zero as it is no longer reported as “not occurring” in the CR data and thus filled from EDGAR.
    • CO2 Emissions in 2.B are much lower because of a drop in emissions from Hydrogen production. For 2022 emissions are even lower because of even lower Hydrogen production.
  • Changes in the TP scenario are very small and mainly come from the updated EI data for energy CO2.

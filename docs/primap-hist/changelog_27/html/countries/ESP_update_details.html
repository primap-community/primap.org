Notes on data changes

Here we list notes explaining important emissions changes for the country.

  • Data from BTR1 has been replaced by data from the 2025 National Inventory Submission. This leads to changes in several sectors and gases which are small in terms of total emissions.
    • The main change for 2023 is a 3.4% reduction in energy CO2 emissions where 2023 growth rates in the CR data are lower than the growth rates in EI 2024 used for extrapolation in PRIMAP-hist v2.6.1.
    • Emissions from the waste sector have changed due to updated activity data.
    • Livestock CH4 emissions are lower due to updated emissions factors for enteric fermentation and for 2023 a drop in emissions not present in the third party data.
    • 2023 emissions for several sectors and gases have changed because data based on extrapolation with third party growth rates has been replaced by country reported data.
    • N2O from M.AG.ELV is higher until 2021 because activity data for animal manure applied to soils has been increased. For 2022 and 2023 emissions are lower because a reduction in inorganic N fertilizer activity data has a higher effect.
  • Changes in the TP scenario are limited to 2023 and come from the updated EI data for energy CO2.

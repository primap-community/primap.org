Notes on data changes

Here we list notes explaining important emissions changes for the country.

  • Data from BTR1 has been replaced by data from the 2025 National Inventory Submission. This leads to changes in several sectors and gases which are small in terms of total emissions.
    • The main change for 2023 is a 2.0% increase in energy CO2 emissions where 2023 growth rates in the CR data are higher than the growth rates in EI2024 used for extrapolation in PRIMAP-hist v2.6.1. EI2025 also has higher growth rates.
    • For cumulative emissions the most important change is that mineral industry emissions are higher for all years. The reason is a higher emissions factor for cement production.
    • CO2 emissions from the IPPU sector are higher because of an emissions increase in several subsectors not present in the third party data used for extrapolation in v2.6.1.
    • CH4 emissions from livestock, waste, and solid fuels (fugitive) are lower because of a drop not present in third party data.
  • Changes in the TP scenario are very small, mostly affect 2023 and mainly come from the updated EI data for energy CO2.

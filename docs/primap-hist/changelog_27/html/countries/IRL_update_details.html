Notes on data changes

Here we list notes explaining important emissions changes for the country.

  • Data from BTR1 has been replaced by data from the 2025 National Inventory Submission. This leads to changes in several sectors and gases which are small in terms of total emissions.
    • The main change for 2023 and cumulative emissions is a 10.2% (2023) / 4.8 (cumulative) reduction in livestock CH4 emissions. The main reason is a change in emissions factors for enteric fermentation mostly for non-dairy cattle and sheep. The emissions factors for N2O from manure management are lower (for non-dairy cattle)
    • Emissions from M.AG.ELV have changed as well, especially for 2023 where the new CR data shows declining emissions which are not present in the data used for extrapolation in v2.6.1.
    • Energy CO2 emissions are reduced for 2023 where growth rates in the CR data are lower than the growth rates in EI 2024 used for extrapolation in PRIMAP-hist v2.6.1
  • Changes in the TP scenario are very small and mainly come from the updated EI data for energy CO2.

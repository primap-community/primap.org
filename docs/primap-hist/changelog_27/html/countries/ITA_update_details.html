Notes on data changes

Here we list notes explaining important emissions changes for the country.

  • Data from BTR1 has been replaced by data from the 2025 National Inventory Submission. This leads to changes in several sectors and gases which are small in terms of total emissions.
    • The main change for 2023 is a 1.5% reduction in energy CO2 emissions where 2023 growth rates in the CR data are lower than the growth rates in EI2024 used for extrapolation in PRIMAP-hist v2.6.1.
    • CO2 from M.AG.ELV is much higher in 2023 because the country reported emissions show a return to 2021 levels while EDGAR data shows a flat emissions profile and thus the EDGAR extrapolated 2023 value used in v2.6.1 is similar to the low 2022 value.
    • N2O from M.AG.ELV in 2023 is higher for the same reason. For all years the time-series is slightly higher because of changed emissions factors for manure management and updated activity data for organic fertilizers.
    • Waste N2O emissions are higher post-2009 because of updated activity data.
    • Waste CH4 is higher in 2023 because emissions are increasing in CR data while EDGAR shows decreasing emissions leading to lower emissions in v2.6.1
    • CH4 in 1.B.2 is lower in 2023 because emissions are decreasing in CR data while EDGAR shows constant emissions leading to higher emissions in v2.6.1
    • HFC emissions are higher after 2012 due to updated activity data.
  • Changes in the TP scenario are very small and mainly come from the updated EI data for energy CO2.
    • 2023 CH4 emissions in 1.B.3 are higher because there exists no third party data and the 2023 data replaces numerical extrapolation.
